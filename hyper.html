Hypertrophy Training

Our Hypertrophy Training program is laser-focused on maximizing muscle growth and achieving a sculpted physique. We utilize scientifically proven methods, including controlled volume, strategic rest periods, and specific rep ranges designed to stimulate hypertrophy. This class is suitable for all levels, from beginners looking to build a foundation to advanced lifters aiming for new mass gains.

  • Structured Volume and Intensity Protocols
  • Focus on Time Under Tension (TUT)
  • Accessory Work for Muscle Shaping
  • Guided Progressive Overload Techniques

Join this class to optimize your body's potential for muscle development under the guidance of our certified specialists.
